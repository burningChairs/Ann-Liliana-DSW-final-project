Welcome to Number Guess!


Log in to play and save your streak!

Number Guess is a fun and engaging web game created by Ann Jordan and Liliana Aguilar.




How To Play



Guess the number in 6 tries!

Each guess must be valid numbers from 0-99.

Once you guess, you get a 'Too High' or 'Too Low' message to help you determine the real number.

If you refresh, your answers won't save!

Come back everyday for a new round!



Examples:



Guess One: 2

Too low


Guess Two: 93

Too High


Guess Three: 84

The guess is correct! This is the secret number.
